Lessons from the badge sitting

  • Exact wrap keeps pixels. It is not a vector. Do not call it a trace.
  • Color regions drop quality. A 3D render will posterize. Say that before download.
  • Edge lines on a photo look terrible if sold as “the original.” Use them as linework, not a substitute.
  • Do not replace a mark the founder already kept.
  • A dark hole plus a white glint reads as an eye. Not on a gem gear.
  • Show the file. Delete the leftover PNG when he said the artefact is out.
